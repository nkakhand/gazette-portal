Comprehensive Comparison

State Gazette vs. Central Gazette of India

Why the Central Gazette (The Gazette of India, Delhi) is legally mandatory and superior for passports, government employment, and nationwide recognition.

Benchmark / Parameter Central Gazette of India (The Gazette of India) State Government Gazette (Local State)
Publishing Authority Department of Publication, Ministry of Housing & Urban Affairs, Govt. of India (Civil Lines, Delhi). Respective State Government Printing Press (e.g., Lucknow, Jaipur, Mumbai).
Territorial Legal Jurisdiction Pan-India & Global: Legally valid across all 28 States, 8 Union Territories, and foreign embassies/Missions worldwide. Primarily limited to the issuing state. Frequently rejected or questioned in other states.
Passport & MEA Acceptance 100% Mandatory & Accepted: Regional Passport Offices (RPO) and MEA insist strictly on the Central Gazette for major name alterations. Frequently rejected or placed under statutory objection by Passport Seva Kendras (PSK).
Central Govt Jobs & Exams Irrefutable proof accepted by UPSC, SSC, Railways (RRB), Armed Forces (Army/Navy/Air Force), and Nationalized Banks (SBI, PNB). May face objections during central recruitment document verification (DV).
EPFO, PAN, Aadhaar & Banking Directly accepted as conclusive identity change evidence nationwide without supplementary court orders. Often requires supplementary affidavits or local sub-divisional approvals.
Digital Access & Verification Published online every Saturday at egazette.gov.in with statutory digital signatures under the IT Act 2000. Varies widely by state; many states lack centralized searchable digital archives.
Legal Verdict: If you are changing your name, correcting spelling, adding a surname, or changing religion/gender, always publish in The Gazette of India (Central Gazette, Delhi). It protects you against future rejections at Passport offices, embassies, universities, or central government exams.
