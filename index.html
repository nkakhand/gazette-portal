Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Clear answers regarding procedures, fees, and submission requirements.

What exactly do I get in the ₹2,500 service?

In the ₹2,500 service, you receive all legally compliant drafting formats delivered directly to your email (Stamp Paper Affidavit text, CD Proforma in .docx format, CD Equivalence Certificate, and the Newspaper Advertisement format). Additionally, you get direct telephonic support on 8118802189 to answer all queries regarding notary, printing, photo attestation, and counter procedure.

Is the Government publishing fee included in ₹2,500?

No. The official Government Gazette fee is strictly separate. Under the updated Department of Publication guidelines, the applicant must physically visit the Civil Lines, Delhi office counter to submit the hardcopy file and pay the government fees directly at the departmental cash/challan counter.

When does the telephone assistance service conclude?

Our dedicated telephonic consultation assists you with every step of preparing and assembling your file. As per our terms, the telephone consultation service concludes once you successfully deposit your physical file at the Civil Lines counter.

Why do I have to go to the Delhi office physically?

As per recent directives from the Department of Publication, in-person physical submission ensures applicant verification, immediate fee receipt generation, and prevents dossier rejections caused by transit postal damages or incomplete submissions.
